Five questions worth asking every installer

  1. What will the system generate a year on my roof, and how did you work it out?
  2. Which panels and inverter are you quoting, and what warranties come with each?
  3. Is scaffolding included, and are there any extra costs for my roof?
  4. Will you register the system with MCS so I can claim Smart Export Guarantee payments?
  5. Would a battery pay for itself on my usage, and what would it add to the price?
