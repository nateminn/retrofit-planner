RetrofitPlanner.co.uk is an independent UK site that helps homeowners work out whether solar panels pay on their own roof, before they ask for a quote. UK organic search only.

Who you would reach: homeowners who have already done the maths. My solar calculator takes their postcode, roof direction and pitch, and shows system size, cost, first-year saving, payback and whether a battery is worth adding. It is backed by solar payback pages for every UK region and a map of solar output for all 361 UK councils, built on PVGIS and government data. The calculator alone drew 13,191 search impressions in 12 months. Anyone who clicks through already knows what solar should save them, which suits a quote you can turn round quickly.

How I would promote you: as the next step on my solar calculator, shown when the result says panels pay, and as a labelled link on my regional solar payback pages and solar guides.
