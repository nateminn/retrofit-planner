Contact Us

Have a question, found an error, or want to suggest an improvement? We'd love to hear from you.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email: hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk

What we can help with

  • Data corrections: if you spot an error in our calculator data or methodology, let us know and we'll investigate promptly.
  • Feature suggestions: ideas for new calculators, improvements to existing tools, or additional data you'd like to see.
  • Methodology questions: journalists, researchers, or anyone wanting to understand how our calculations work. See our methodology page for published details.
  • Partnership enquiries: MCS-certified installers, energy companies, or organisations wanting to work with us.

What we cannot help with

We are a calculator and information site, not an installation company, so we cannot give a personalised energy assessment, help with a grant application (contact the scheme administrator directly) or give detailed technical advice about a specific installation. If you would like quotes, use the form below: we pass your request to up to three certified installers covering your postcode.

Response time

We aim to respond to all emails within two working days. Data correction reports are prioritised.
