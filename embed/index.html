Put our calculators on your own site

Our heat pump cost calculator is free to embed on any website. One line of HTML, no account, no cookies and no tracking scripts loaded on your readers. All we ask is that you keep the credit link.

What your readers get

The widget below is the live embed, running exactly as it would on your page. It uses the same model as our full heat pump calculator, so the figures never diverge, and it is updated whenever the Ofgem cap or the grant changes.

Copy the code

Paste this wherever you want the calculator to appear. The small script keeps the height right when someone runs a calculation, so there is never a scrollbar inside the box.

<iframe
  src="https://www.retrofitplanner.co.uk/embed/heat-pump-calculator/"
  title="Heat pump cost calculator"
  loading="lazy"
  style="width:100%;max-width:640px;height:560px;
         border:1px solid #e5e3dc;border-radius:12px">
</iframe>
<p style="font-size:13px">Calculator by
  <a href="https://www.retrofitplanner.co.uk/heat-pump-calculator/">
    Retrofit Planner</a>
</p>

<script>
addEventListener("message", function (e) {
  if (e.origin !== "https://www.retrofitplanner.co.uk") return;
  if (!e.data || !e.data.rpHeight) return;
  var f = document.querySelectorAll(
    'iframe[src*="retrofitplanner.co.uk/embed/"]');
  for (var i = 0; i < f.length; i++) {
    if (f[i].contentWindow === e.source) {
      f[i].style.height = e.data.rpHeight + "px";
    }
  }
});
</script>

The terms, in plain English

  • It is free. Commercial sites included. No account, no key, no usage limit.
  • Keep the credit link. The line under the iframe, pointing to the calculator, is the whole price. Leave it as a normal link that search engines can follow.
  • Nothing tracks your readers. The widget sets no cookies, loads no analytics and makes no third-party requests. It needs no mention in your cookie banner.
  • Do not present it as your own. You can wrap it in your own page and write your own introduction, but do not remove the attribution or claim the model.
  • We keep it current. When the price cap or the grant changes we update it, and your copy updates with it. If we ever retire a widget we will leave a redirect rather than break your page.

Frequently asked questions

Is the calculator really free to embed?

Yes, on any site including commercial ones. There is no account, no API key and no limit on page views. The only condition is that you keep the credit link under the widget.

Will it slow my page down?

No. The widget is about 11 KB with no external requests, no fonts and no analytics, and the snippet sets it to load lazily so it does not download until a reader scrolls near it.

Do I need to mention it in my cookie banner?

No. The widget sets no cookies, uses no storage and loads nothing from third parties, so there is nothing to consent to.

Can I change the colours or the wording?

Not directly, because everyone shares one copy and that is how updates reach you automatically. You can set the width and the border in the snippet, and it follows your reader's light or dark mode. Ask us on the contact page if you need something specific.

What happens when energy prices change?

We update the model and every embed updates with it, because your page loads the current version rather than a copy. The figures here use the Ofgem cap for October to December 2026 and the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant.

Will the numbers match your own calculator?

Yes. The widget runs the same model as the full calculator and we test them against each other, so a reader who follows the link sees the same figures with more detail around them.
