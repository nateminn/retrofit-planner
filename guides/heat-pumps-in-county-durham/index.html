Heat pumps in County Durham

The short answer

  • 637 heat pumps installed in County Durham with a government grant since May 2022
  • 27.1 for every 10,000 households, ranked 175th of 318 councils in England and Wales
  • 234 grants in 2025/26, against 86 in 2023/24

Updated 25 September 2026. Every figure on this page comes from official statistics; see where our data comes from.

637 heat pumps have been installed in County Durham with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant since May 2022, 27.1 for every 10,000 households. That ranks County Durham 175th of 318 councils in England and Wales, below the England and Wales figure of 35.0.

Getting a heat pump in County Durham

£7,500 off, or £9,000 replacing oil or LPG in a home with no mains gas, until 31 March 2027. A typical 3 bed semi costs £11,000 to £15,500 installed before the grant.

In England, ask your council whether it has Warm Homes: Local Grant funding, which can pay for insulation and a heat pump for households on lower incomes. See what a heat pump would cost and save in your home with the heat pump calculator, and what it costs to run with the running cost calculator.

How we worked this out

Heat pumps are Boiler Upgrade Scheme grants paid from 23 May 2022 to 30 June 2026 (DESNZ Boiler Upgrade Scheme statistics, August 2026, Tables Q1.2 and A1.7), counting air and ground source heat pumps. Heat pumps installed without the grant, such as in most new build and social homes, are not counted. Households are the Census 2021 count for County Durham: 234,761.

Frequently asked questions

How many heat pumps are there in County Durham?

637 heat pumps have been installed in County Durham with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant since the scheme opened in May 2022, to the end of June 2026. That is 27.1 for every 10,000 households.

How does County Durham compare with the rest of the country?

County Durham ranks 175th of 318 councils in England and Wales for heat pump grants per household, below the England and Wales figure of 35.0. Across the North East the figure is 22.4.

Can I get a heat pump grant in County Durham?

Yes, if you own the home and are replacing fossil fuel or electric heating: the Boiler Upgrade Scheme gives £7,500 off a heat pump, or £9,000 replacing oil or LPG in a home with no mains gas, until 31 March 2027. Your MCS installer applies for it. In England, ask your council whether it has Warm Homes: Local Grant funding, which can pay for insulation and a heat pump for households on lower incomes.
