Heat pumps in Manchester

The short answer

  • 320 heat pumps installed in Manchester with a government grant since May 2022
  • 14.9 for every 10,000 households, ranked 255th of 318 councils in England and Wales
  • 112 grants in 2025/26, against 37 in 2023/24

Updated 25 September 2026. Every figure on this page comes from official statistics; see where our data comes from.

320 heat pumps have been installed in Manchester with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant since May 2022, 14.9 for every 10,000 households. That ranks Manchester 255th of 318 councils in England and Wales, below the England and Wales figure of 35.0.

Getting a heat pump in Manchester

£7,500 off, or £9,000 replacing oil or LPG in a home with no mains gas, until 31 March 2027. A typical 3 bed semi costs £11,000 to £15,500 installed before the grant.

In England, ask your council whether it has Warm Homes: Local Grant funding, which can pay for insulation and a heat pump for households on lower incomes. See what a heat pump would cost and save in your home with the heat pump calculator, and what it costs to run with the running cost calculator.

How we worked this out

Heat pumps are Boiler Upgrade Scheme grants paid from 23 May 2022 to 30 June 2026 (DESNZ Boiler Upgrade Scheme statistics, August 2026, Tables Q1.2 and A1.7), counting air and ground source heat pumps. Heat pumps installed without the grant, such as in most new build and social homes, are not counted. Households are the Census 2021 count for Manchester: 214,732.

Frequently asked questions

How many heat pumps are there in Manchester?

320 heat pumps have been installed in Manchester with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant since the scheme opened in May 2022, to the end of June 2026. That is 14.9 for every 10,000 households.

How does Manchester compare with the rest of the country?

Manchester ranks 255th of 318 councils in England and Wales for heat pump grants per household, below the England and Wales figure of 35.0. Across the North West the figure is 22.6.

Can I get a heat pump grant in Manchester?

Yes, if you own the home and are replacing fossil fuel or electric heating: the Boiler Upgrade Scheme gives £7,500 off a heat pump, or £9,000 replacing oil or LPG in a home with no mains gas, until 31 March 2027. Your MCS installer applies for it. In England, ask your council whether it has Warm Homes: Local Grant funding, which can pay for insulation and a heat pump for households on lower incomes.
