Home Upgrade Grant (HUG2): Closed, and What Replaced It

The Home Upgrade Grant closed in March 2025. It is no longer open to applications, and GOV.UK now points to its replacement, the Warm Homes: Local Grant, which you apply for through your local council. This page is kept as a record of what HUG2 covered and who qualified, because the successor targets a similar group. Check what is actually open now.

The Home Upgrade Grant funded insulation and low carbon heating for low-income households in homes not connected to the mains gas grid in England. Phase 2 had up to £630 million, was delivered by councils from 2023 until March 2025, and upgraded 11,900 homes with 22,000 measures. Unlike ECO4, which energy suppliers fund, HUG2 was funded by the government. Costs were capped per home and averaged across each council's project, so no household was entitled to a fixed sum. Its replacement, the Warm Homes: Local Grant, is open now in England, and it covers homes on the gas grid as well as off it.

ECO4 closes on 31 December 2026. The work has to be finished by then, not just applied for, and installers fill up as the date gets closer. Government has confirmed there will be no successor supplier obligation: from 2027 the money moves to council run schemes under the Warm Homes Plan, which you apply for through your local authority rather than your energy supplier. If you think you qualify, start now. Check what you qualify for.

HUG2 used a whole-house approach, and the Warm Homes: Local Grant does the same. A retrofit coordinator assesses your property and designs a package of measures tailored to your home, so you do not choose individual measures.

Heat pumps are funded

Like HUG2, the Warm Homes: Local Grant can fund an air source heat pump where suitable, on or off the gas grid, with a per-home cap of £16,600 for low carbon heating averaged across the council's project. If you currently heat with oil or LPG, a heat pump ends your fuel deliveries. See our heat pump running costs guide for the savings comparison.

How to apply

HUG2 is closed, so these are the steps for its replacement, the Warm Homes: Local Grant.

Step 1: Apply through GOV.UK or your council's website. 271 councils took part, over 97% of those eligible in England, though funding can run out locally. If yours has none left, you may still qualify for ECO4 or the Boiler Upgrade Scheme. The GOV.UK helpline for the online service is 0800 098 7950.

Step 2: The council usually contacts you within 10 working days to get more information and arrange a home survey. It checks your income, benefits or postcode, and the home's EPC.

Step 3: A qualified retrofit assessor visits your home and records everything needed for the whole-house plan: insulation levels, heating system, windows, ventilation, and building fabric.

Step 4: A retrofit coordinator designs your improvement package and arranges installation through the council's approved contractor network. All work must meet PAS 2035 quality standards and be delivered by TrustMark-registered installers.

Step 5: Installation happens. If you own the home you live in, you pay nothing. If you rent, the landlord gets one home fully funded and pays half the cost of any others.

Step 6: A new EPC is produced showing your improved rating. The retrofit coordinator signs off the work and provides a handover pack with warranties and documentation.

Check for scams

The Warm Homes: Local Grant, like HUG2 before it, is delivered through your local council. Anyone still offering you HUG2 is out of date at best. Never pay money for an assessment or application. If someone contacts you offering "free government insulation" and asks for a fee, it is a scam. Verify any approach through your council's website.

Frequently asked questions

What was the Home Upgrade Grant?

A government scheme, run by councils in England, that funded insulation and low carbon heating for low-income households in off gas grid homes rated D to G. Phase 2 had up to £630 million and closed in March 2025, having upgraded 11,900 homes. The Warm Homes: Local Grant replaced it and covers homes on and off the gas grid.

Who qualified for HUG2?

Off gas grid homes in England rated D to G, in a council area taking part, where the household had a gross income of up to £36,000 (£31,000 before April 2024), claimed a means-tested benefit, or lived in an eligible low income postcode. Owner occupiers and tenants could apply; private landlords paid at least a third of the cost, and social landlords at least half.

Is the Home Upgrade Grant still open?

No. HUG2 closed in March 2025 and GOV.UK now points to the Warm Homes: Local Grant instead. That is open in England through councils until 31 March 2028 for households with income below £36,000, on a listed benefit or in an eligible postcode, in homes rated D to G. Use our grant checker.

What does the Warm Homes: Local Grant cover?

Insulation, solar panels, smart controls and an air source heat pump where suitable, chosen after a home survey. Owner occupiers pay nothing. Per-home cost caps of £16,600 for energy performance measures and £16,600 for low carbon heating apply, averaged across each council's project, so they are not an amount any household is entitled to.

How do I apply now?

Apply for the Warm Homes: Local Grant through GOV.UK or your council's website. The council usually contacts you within 10 working days to arrange a home survey. Use our grant checker first.
