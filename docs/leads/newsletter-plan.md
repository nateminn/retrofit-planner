# Email updates: plan and first issue

The footer on every page now asks for an email address for "a short email when something
changes that affects your home". People have been promised exactly that, so send one when
something genuinely changes, and not otherwise. An empty list is better than a spammy one.

Sign-ups arrive in the Netlify form "newsletter" with the page, the consent wording and
its version. Export the list from Netlify as CSV and import it into a free-tier email
tool with an unsubscribe link and a UK or EU data location. Do not use any tool connected
to icons.com.

## When to send

- The Ofgem price cap announcement for each new quarter (late February, May, August and
  November), with what it means for a typical home and for heat pump running costs.
- A grant opening, closing or changing (the Boiler Upgrade Scheme, Warm Homes grants).
- A rule change for homes or landlords (for example minimum EPC standards).
