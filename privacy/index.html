Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version

Retrofit Planner is a free UK home energy site. Our calculators run in your browser and the figures you type in are never sent to us or stored. We use Google Analytics to see which pages are read, and some links earn us a commission. We do not show advertising. The site sets no cookies. We never sell your data.

Who we are

Retrofit Planner is operated from the United Kingdom and is the data controller for any personal data collected through this site. You can reach us at hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Calculator inputs

Everything you enter into a calculator (property type, bedrooms, heating system, bills and so on) is processed by JavaScript in your browser. It is not transmitted to our servers, not stored and not shared. When you leave the page it is gone.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, what device and browser they use and their approximate location. Google truncates IP addresses and we do not combine analytics data with anything that identifies you. Because no cookie or similar identifier is stored on your device, PECR's consent rule does not bite, and our lawful basis for the limited data Google does receive is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site performs. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising

We do not currently show any advertising. There are no ad scripts on this site and no advertising cookies are set. If that ever changes we will say so here before it does, and we would ask for your agreement first.

Newsletter

We have removed the newsletter sign-up form, so no new email addresses are being collected. A small number of addresses given through the old form are still held by Netlify. Nothing has ever been sent to them. We keep them on the consent you gave at the time, and if we do start sending updates they will only be the updates you asked for. You can ask us to delete your address at any point and we will.

Quote requests

If you ask for heat pump quotes we collect your postcode, property type, current heating, timing, name, email address and an optional phone number. We use them to respond to your request. Only if you tick the box on the form do we pass your details to up to three installers, who may then contact you directly and who are responsible for their own use of your data. Requests are stored by Netlify, which hosts the form, and deleted after 12 months. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw by emailing us.

Contact

If you email us we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your query, and you can ask for it to be deleted.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site earn us a commission. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, and we are a member of the Awin affiliate network, through which we may be paid when you go on to buy or request a quote from a company we link to. It never costs you more.

Any link that earns us something is marked as sponsored in the page code and carries a note saying so at the point you click it. Links to government pages, regulators and certification bodies never earn us anything.

What we will not do is let the commission choose the advice. Every figure on this site comes from the same published model whatever you decide, and the calculators pick which next step to show you from their own result: where a heat pump would cost you more to run than your current heating, the page says so and points you at a cheaper tariff rather than at quotes. When you follow any link the destination site may set its own cookies, which we do not control.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies at all. Not for analytics, not for advertising, and not to remember a cookie choice, because there is no choice to remember.

Google Analytics runs here, but it is configured with consent mode set to denied before it loads, which stops it writing anything to your browser. It counts the page view and nothing else. It cannot tell that two page views came from the same person, it cannot follow you between visits, and it leaves no identifier behind. We also use no local storage and no session storage.

We chose this deliberately. Measuring which pages get read does not require knowing who read them, and it seemed better to collect less than to put a consent banner in front of every page.

You can still stop Google Analytics seeing your visit entirely, with Google's own opt out add on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, with your browser's tracking protection, or with any content blocker.

Third-party services

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font files are requested. Google Analytics is described above. We do not control how these providers process data beyond the settings we choose; their own privacy policies apply.

How long we keep data

Analytics reports are retained in Google Analytics for 14 months. Addresses from the withdrawn newsletter form are kept until you ask us to delete yours, and no new ones are collected. Contact emails are kept for as long as the query is open and then deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy in a portable form. To exercise any of these rights email hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This site is written for adult homeowners and tenants and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our use of data changes and show the date of the last change at the top. Material changes, such as adding a new third-party service, will be reflected here before they take effect.
