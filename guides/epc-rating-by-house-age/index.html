EPC rating by age of home

Older homes are far more likely to have solid walls. 85% of homes built before 1919 have them and 46% of those built 1919 to 1944; solid walls lose far more heat than filled cavity walls.

Frequently asked questions

Do older houses have worse EPC ratings?

Yes. In the English Housing Survey 2024, 27% of homes built before 1919 are band C or better, against 94% of homes built after 2013. The average rating rises from 60 points for the oldest homes to 78 for the newest.

Which homes use the most gas?

Homes built between 1919 and 1944, a median 11,745 kWh a year and 122 kWh per square metre in houses, slightly more than homes built before 1919 (11,007 kWh and 110 kWh). Homes built since 2012 use a median 7,181 kWh.
