RetrofitPlanner.co.uk is an independent UK home energy site running seven calculators, including a boiler versus heat pump comparison built on the DESNZ National Energy Efficiency Data framework rather than generic averages. It tells visitors plainly when a heat pump will not save them money, which is why people trust the recommendation when it does.

The site takes about 430 organic clicks a month from Google, weighted towards heat pump and EPC queries, and is growing steadily.

Heatable covers boilers, heat pumps and solar, which maps onto three of the calculators.
