Great British Insulation Scheme (GBIS): Closed on 31 March 2026

The Great British Insulation Scheme closed on 31 March 2026 and takes no new applications. It ran from April 2023 and paid for one insulation measure, usually loft or cavity wall, for homes in Council Tax bands A to D in England (A to E in Scotland and Wales) with an EPC of D or below, and unlike ECO4 nobody in the house had to be on benefits. By the end it had installed 139,100 measures in 100,900 homes. This page keeps the detail of what GBIS covered, because some companies still mention it, and sets out what is still open in 2026.

Who qualifies

GBIS had two groups.

General Group (no benefits required)

RequirementDetail
Council Tax bandA to D in England (A to E in Scotland and Wales)
EPC ratingD, E, F, or G
Benefits required?No
Property typeOwner-occupied or privately rented (with landlord permission)

This was the key difference from ECO4: the General Group had no income or benefits test.

Low-Income Group

Households on a means-tested benefit, broadly the same qualifying benefits as ECO4, could use the Low-Income Group whatever their Council Tax band.

What GBIS covers

GBIS funded single measures rather than the whole-house approach of ECO4. Cavity wall insulation made up 37% of the 139,100 measures installed, loft insulation 28% and heating controls 25%. The table shows what the main insulation measures save, as the government has measured it in real homes.

With GBIS closed, ECO4 is the supplier-funded route left, until 31 December 2026.

GBIS closed on 31 March 2026

The scheme ended as planned. Nothing replaced it like for like: its insulation work folds into the government's Warm Homes Plan, the £15 billion programme published in January 2026. ECO4 still runs to 31 December 2026 for households on qualifying benefits. Check what you qualify for with our grant eligibility checker.

What you can still get in 2026

GBIS is closed, but three routes to free or subsidised insulation are open, and a fourth is coming.

SchemeWho it is forWhat it pays forDeadline

Scheme rules from GOV.UK and Ofgem, checked 25 September 2026. The Warm Homes: Local Grant is run by councils, so both the eligibility route and the waiting list depend on where you live.

Step 1: Check your EPC rating on GOV.UK. Every remaining scheme is aimed at homes rated D or below, and the certificate is what an installer works from.

Step 2: Run our grant eligibility checker. It covers ECO4, the Boiler Upgrade Scheme, the Warm Homes: Local Grant and the Scottish, Welsh and Northern Irish schemes in one pass.

Step 3: If you are on a qualifying benefit, contact your energy supplier or council about ECO4. If you are not, and you live in England, apply for the Warm Homes: Local Grant through GOV.UK or your council, and ask whether the council can refer you into ECO4 under flexible eligibility.

Step 4: If nothing fits, price the work yourself. The Energy Saving Trust puts loft insulation at about £750 for a bare loft and cavity wall insulation at about £2,700. On measured savings both take many years to pay back at full price, which is why the funded routes matter, though a bare loft saves more than the typical figure. Frequently asked questions

Did I need to be on benefits for GBIS?

No. The General Group was open to homes in Council Tax bands A to D in England, or A to E in Scotland and Wales, with an EPC of D or below. That was the key difference from ECO4. The scheme closed on 31 March 2026.

Is GBIS still open?

No. The Great British Insulation Scheme closed on 31 March 2026 and no new applications are accepted. The Warm Homes Plan brings supplier schemes to a close. ECO4 runs to 31 December 2026, and in England the Warm Homes: Local Grant runs through councils until 31 March 2028.

What replaced the Great British Insulation Scheme?

Nothing replaced it like for like. For households on benefits, ECO4 runs until 31 December 2026. In England, the Warm Homes: Local Grant funds insulation for households under £36,000 gross, on a listed benefit or in an eligible postcode, through councils. Use our grant checker.
