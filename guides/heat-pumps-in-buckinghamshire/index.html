Heat pumps in Buckinghamshire

The short answer

  • 1,288 heat pumps installed in Buckinghamshire with a government grant since May 2022
  • 58.5 for every 10,000 households, ranked 76th of 318 councils in England and Wales
  • 443 grants in 2025/26, against 241 in 2023/24

Updated 25 September 2026. Every figure on this page comes from our published model; see where our data comes from.

1,288 heat pumps have been installed in Buckinghamshire with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant since May 2022, 58.5 for every 10,000 households. That ranks Buckinghamshire 76th of 318 councils in England and Wales, 1.7 times the England and Wales figure of 35.0.

Getting a heat pump in Buckinghamshire

£7,500 off, or £9,000 replacing oil or LPG until 31 March 2027. A typical 3 bed semi costs £11,000 to £15,500 installed before the grant.

How we worked this out

Heat pumps are Boiler Upgrade Scheme grants paid from 23 May 2022 to 30 June 2026 (DESNZ Boiler Upgrade Scheme statistics, August 2026, Tables Q1.2 and A1.7), counting air and ground source heat pumps. Heat pumps installed without the grant, such as in most new build and social homes, are not counted. Households are the Census 2021 count for Buckinghamshire: 220,328.

Frequently asked questions

How many heat pumps are there in Buckinghamshire?

1,288 heat pumps have been installed in Buckinghamshire with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant since the scheme opened in May 2022, to the end of June 2026. That is 58.5 for every 10,000 households.

How does Buckinghamshire compare with the rest of the country?

Buckinghamshire ranks 76th of 318 councils in England and Wales for heat pump grants per household, 1.7 times the England and Wales figure of 35.0. Across the South East the figure is 45.8.

Can I get a heat pump grant in Buckinghamshire?

Yes, if you own the home and are replacing fossil fuel or electric heating: the Boiler Upgrade Scheme gives £7,500 off a heat pump, or £9,000 replacing oil or LPG until 31 March 2027. Your MCS installer applies for it. In England, ask your council whether it has Warm Homes: Local Grant funding, which can pay for insulation and a heat pump for households on lower incomes.
