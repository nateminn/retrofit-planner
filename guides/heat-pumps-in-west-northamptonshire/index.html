How we worked this out

Heat pumps are Boiler Upgrade Scheme grants paid from 23 May 2022 to 30 June 2026 (DESNZ Boiler Upgrade Scheme statistics, August 2026, Tables Q1.2 and A1.7), counting air and ground source heat pumps. Heat pumps installed without the grant, such as in most new build and social homes, are not counted. Households are the Census 2021 count for West Northamptonshire: 172,641.

Frequently asked questions

How many heat pumps are there in West Northamptonshire?

750 heat pumps have been installed in West Northamptonshire with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant since the scheme opened in May 2022, to the end of June 2026. That is 43.4 for every 10,000 households.

How does West Northamptonshire compare with the rest of the country?

West Northamptonshire ranks 109th of 318 councils in England and Wales for heat pump grants per household, 1.2 times the England and Wales figure of 35.0. Across the East Midlands the figure is 41.8.

Can I get a heat pump grant in West Northamptonshire?

Yes, if you own the home and are replacing fossil fuel or electric heating: the Boiler Upgrade Scheme gives £7,500 off a heat pump, or £9,000 replacing oil or LPG until 31 March 2027. Your MCS installer applies for it. In England, ask your council whether it has Warm Homes: Local Grant funding, which can pay for insulation and a heat pump for households on lower incomes.
