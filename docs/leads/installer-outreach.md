# Installer outreach

Use only facts that are true on the day you send. Do not claim numbers of leads,
customers or areas you have not got.

## First contact

Subject: A homeowner near [town] has asked for a heat pump quote

Hi [name],

I run Retrofit Planner (retrofitplanner.co.uk), a free UK site that shows homeowners what
a heat pump, insulation or solar would cost and save for their own house, using
government data.

A homeowner in [postcode district] has asked for quotes for [a heat pump in a 3 bed semi
heated by gas, looking to install within three months] and has agreed to be contacted by
up to three MCS-certified installers. I checked your certification on the MCS register
today and would like to pass the request to you.

There is no charge for this one. If it is useful, I would like to talk about sending you
more requests from your area and what would work for you.

Reply and I will send the details straight over.

[Your name]
Retrofit Planner

## If they want more

Say what is true: how many requests have come from their area so far (count them in the
tracker), that each person has ticked a box agreeing to be contacted by up to three
certified installers who may pay for the introduction, and that you never pass a request
to more than three businesses. Ask what they pay other lead sources before naming a
price.
