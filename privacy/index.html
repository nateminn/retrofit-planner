Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version

Retrofit Planner is a free UK home energy site. Our calculators run in your browser and the figures you type in are never sent to us or stored. We use Google Analytics to see which pages are read, we show advertising through an advertising partner, and some links earn us a commission. Advertising and analytics cookies are only set once you have agreed to them through the cookie banner. We never sell your data.

Who we are

Retrofit Planner is operated from the United Kingdom and is the data controller for any personal data collected through this site. You can reach us at hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Calculator inputs

Everything you enter into a calculator (property type, bedrooms, heating system, bills and so on) is processed by JavaScript in your browser. It is not transmitted to our servers, not stored and not shared. When you leave the page it is gone.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, what device and browser they use and their approximate location. Google truncates IP addresses and we do not combine analytics data with anything that identifies you. Our lawful basis is your consent for the cookies involved, and our legitimate interest in understanding how the site performs. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. While our advertising partner evaluates the site it may also run its own audience measurement script, which collects the same kind of aggregate usage data.

Advertising

We show advertising provided by an advertising partner, currently Journey by Mediavine or Google AdSense. The partner and the advertisers it works with use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, to measure them and, where you have agreed, to personalise them based on your visits to this and other websites. Personalised advertising is only switched on for visitors who consent through the cookie banner; without consent you will see non-personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings, and from other vendors at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com.

Newsletter

We have removed the newsletter sign-up form, so no new email addresses are being collected. A small number of addresses given through the old form are still held by Netlify. Nothing has ever been sent to them. We keep them on the consent you gave at the time, and if we do start sending updates they will only be the updates you asked for. You can ask us to delete your address at any point and we will.

Quote requests

If you ask for heat pump quotes we collect your postcode, property type, current heating, timing, name, email address and an optional phone number. We use them to respond to your request. Only if you tick the box on the form do we pass your details to up to three installers, who may then contact you directly and who are responsible for their own use of your data. Requests are stored by Netlify, which hosts the form, and deleted after 12 months. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw by emailing us.

Contact

If you email us we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your query, and you can ask for it to be deleted.

Affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases made through links on this site. Other links, such as installer finders and government pages, are not affiliate links. When you follow any link the destination site may set its own cookies, which we do not control.

Cookies

We use three kinds of cookies and similar technologies.

  • Strictly necessary: a single cookie that records your cookie choices so we do not ask you again. This is set without consent because the site cannot respect your choice without it.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics cookies (names beginning _ga) that distinguish visitors and sessions. They last up to two years and are only set with your consent.
  • Advertising: cookies set by our advertising partner and the advertisers it works with to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them if you agree. They last up to thirteen months and are only set with your consent.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by reopening the cookie banner from the link in the footer of every page, and you can clear cookies through your browser settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything already done on the basis of it.

Third-party services

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font files are requested. Google Analytics and our advertising partner are described above. We do not control how these providers process data beyond the settings we choose; their own privacy policies apply.

How long we keep data

Analytics reports are retained in Google Analytics for 14 months. Addresses from the withdrawn newsletter form are kept until you ask us to delete yours, and no new ones are collected. Contact emails are kept for as long as the query is open and then deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy in a portable form. To exercise any of these rights email hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This site is written for adult homeowners and tenants and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our use of data changes and show the date of the last change at the top. Material changes, such as adding a new third-party service, will be reflected here before they take effect.
