Five questions worth asking every installer

  1. Will you survey the property before quoting, including for damp and ventilation?
  2. Which material and thickness are you quoting, and why that one for my home?
  3. Is the work covered by a guarantee, and who provides it?
  4. Could any of the cost be covered by a grant, and will you help apply?
  5. How long will the work take, and what do I need to do beforehand?
