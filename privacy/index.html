Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version

Retrofit Planner is a free UK home energy site. Our calculators run in your browser, and we never store the figures you type in. We use Google Analytics to see which pages are read. The site sets no cookies and shows no advertising. If you ask for quotes and tick the box to agree, we pass your details to up to three certified installers, who may pay us for the introduction. If you sign up for email updates, we use your address only to send them. We never share your details with anyone else.

Who we are

Retrofit Planner is operated from the United Kingdom and is the data controller for any personal data collected through this site. You can reach us at hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Calculator inputs

Everything you enter into a calculator (property type, bedrooms, heating system, bills and so on) is processed by JavaScript in your browser, and we do not store it. After you calculate, your answers are written into the page address so the result can be kept or shared. That address stays in your browser history, and if you reload the page, open the link or share it, it travels like any web address: our host Netlify records it in its access logs and Google Analytics records the page address. The answers describe a home, not a person, and we never combine them with anything that identifies you.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, what device and browser they use and their approximate location. Google truncates IP addresses and we do not combine analytics data with anything that identifies you. Because no cookie or similar identifier is stored on your device, PECR's consent rule does not bite, and our lawful basis for the limited data Google does receive is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site performs. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising

We do not currently show any advertising. There are no ad scripts on this site and no advertising cookies are set. If that ever changes we will say so here before it does, and we would ask for your agreement first.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates we collect your email address, the page you signed up on and the wording you agreed to. We use them only to email you about changes to UK home energy rules, grants and prices. We never share or sell your email address. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time by emailing us. Our lawful basis is your consent. Addresses given through our earlier sign-up form are kept on the consent given at the time and treated the same way.

Quote requests

If you ask for quotes we collect your postcode, property details, the work you want and when, your name, email address and an optional phone number, along with the page you asked on and the exact wording you agreed to.

A request can only be sent once you tick the box agreeing that we may pass your details to up to three certified installers or energy assessors covering your postcode, who may then contact you by email or phone about a quote. We only pass requests to businesses holding the certification the work needs: MCS for heat pumps and solar, TrustMark for insulation, and an accreditation scheme for EPC assessors. Installers may pay us a fee for each introduction. It costs you nothing and never changes any figure on this site.

Once an installer has your details, they are responsible for their own use of them. We keep your request, and the record of what you agreed to, for 12 months and then delete it. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk. If you do, we will not pass your details on, and we will ask any installer we have already passed them to to delete them. Requests and sign-ups are stored by Netlify, which hosts this site and its forms.

Contact

If you email us we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your query, and you can ask for it to be deleted.

Links to other sites

When you follow a link to another site, that site may set its own cookies, which we do not control. No link on this site earns us anything.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies at all. Not for analytics, not for advertising, and not to remember a cookie choice, because there is no choice to remember.

Google Analytics runs here, but it is configured with consent mode set to denied before it loads, which stops it writing anything to your browser. It counts page views, and when you send a form it records that a sign-up or quote request happened, without any of your details. It cannot tell that two page views came from the same person, it cannot follow you between visits, and it leaves no identifier behind. We also use no local storage and no session storage.

We chose this deliberately. Measuring which pages get read does not require knowing who read them, and it seemed better to collect less than to put a consent banner in front of every page.

You can still stop Google Analytics seeing your visit entirely, with Google's own opt out add on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, with your browser's tracking protection, or with any content blocker.

Third-party services

The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability. Fonts are served from this site, so no font provider sees your visit. Google Analytics is described above. We do not control how these providers process data beyond the settings we choose; their own privacy policies apply.

How long we keep data

Google Analytics keeps detailed event data for no more than 14 months. Email addresses for updates are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete yours. Contact emails are kept for as long as the query is open and then deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive a copy in a portable form. To exercise any of these rights email hello@retrofitplanner.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This site is written for adult homeowners and tenants and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our use of data changes and show the date of the last change at the top. Material changes, such as adding a new third-party service, will be reflected here before they take effect.
