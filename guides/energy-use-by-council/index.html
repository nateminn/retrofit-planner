The data behind this table

Typical costs are the medians priced at the Ofgem cap for October to December 2026, with standing charges. Compare your own bill with the energy bill calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much gas does a typical home use?

A median 10,012 kWh a year across England and Wales, among homes with a gas meter, and 2,538 kWh of electricity, from 2024 meter readings. At the October to December 2026 price cap that is about £906 for gas and £868 for electricity, including standing charges.

Which council uses the most gas?

Harrow, where the median home with gas uses 14,234 kWh a year, followed by Barnet and Elmbridge.

Why is gas use low in Cornwall?

Homes of every type in Cornwall use less gas than the England and Wales median: a semi-detached house 6,972 kWh against 10,574. The figure covers only homes with a gas meter, and many Cornish homes are off the gas grid; Cornwall also has the 3rd highest rate of Boiler Upgrade Scheme heat pumps per household of any council.
