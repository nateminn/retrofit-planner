How much does it cost to insulate a terraced house?

The short answer

  • £500 to £650 for loft insulation and £1,100 for cavity walls in a mid-terrace
  • About £94 a year saved by cavity wall insulation in a 3 bed terrace on gas, a 12 year payback at full price
  • £12,000 to £15,000 for solid walls, common in older terraces, which pays best with a grant

Updated 25 September 2026. Every figure on this page comes from our published model; see where our data comes from.

Insulating a terraced house costs from about £500 to top up the loft to £12,000 or more for solid walls. Cavity wall insulation costs about £1,100, the Energy Saving Trust's figure for a mid-terrace house, and homes like yours measurably used 12.0% less gas afterwards.

What each measure costs

Loft first, then the walls. For a 3 bed terraced house on gas, cavity wall insulation saves about £94 a year and pays back in 12 years; solid walls save more but cost far more.

Cavity or solid walls?

Check before you price anything. Homes built from the 1930s usually have cavity walls, which are far cheaper to insulate; brickwork showing the short ends of some bricks means solid walls. Your EPC records the wall type.

Solid wall insulation costs about £12,000 fitted inside or £15,000 outside, and saves about £135 a year here, so at full price it only pays with a grant. Read is cavity wall insulation worth it and solid wall insulation cost.

Loft and cavity together

About £1,750 for both, saving about £65 a year. Homes that had loft and cavity wall insulation fitted together used a median 8.4% less gas, less than the two separate figures added up.

In our EPC model the loft adds about 7 points and cavity walls about 8, often enough to lift a home a band. Insulating first also shrinks the heat pump a 3 bed terraced house needs, from 8 kW at average insulation to 7 kW well insulated. Grants that can pay for it

ECO4 until 31 December 2026, then council grants. ECO4 funds insulation for households on qualifying benefits, in homes rated D to G if owned or E to G if privately rented, and the work must be finished by then.

In England a Warm Homes: Local Grant through your council can help households on lower incomes, and insulation carries 0% VAT until 31 March 2027. Landlords can check the 2030 band C rules with the landlord EPC calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to insulate a terraced house?

From about £500 to top up the loft to £12,000 for internal solid wall insulation, at Energy Saving Trust prices before any grant. Cavity wall insulation costs about £1,100 for a mid-terrace house.

Is cavity wall insulation worth it for a terraced house?

Homes measurably used 12.0% less gas after it, about £94 a year for a 3 bed terraced house on gas, so at about £1,100 it takes 12 years to pay back at full price. It is often free through a grant, and it also adds about 8 EPC points in our model.

Can I get insulation for my terraced house free?

Possibly. ECO4 funds insulation for households on qualifying benefits until 31 December 2026, in homes rated D to G if owned or E to G if privately rented, and in England a Warm Homes: Local Grant through the council can help households on lower incomes. Insulation carries 0% VAT until 31 March 2027.
