Average energy bill for a 2 bed house

Updated 25 September 2026. Every figure on this page comes from our published model; see how accurate it is.

A 2 bed terrace with average insulation and gas central heating costs about £1,436 a year, or £120 a month, at the Ofgem price cap for October to December 2026. That is about £666 for gas and £770 for electricity, both including their standing charges.

Average bill by type of 2 bed home

Electricity is the larger part of the bill in a home this size, and gas use depends on the size and type of the home. Heating use comes from government meter data for 39,502 gas heated homes; electricity for lights, appliances and cooking is scaled by home size from Ofgem's typical consumption figures.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average energy bill for a 2 bed house?

About £1,436 a year, or £120 a month, for a 2 bed terrace with average insulation heated by mains gas, at the Ofgem price cap for October to December 2026. That includes both standing charges.

How much gas does a 2 bed house use?

A 2 bed terrace with average insulation uses about 7,000 kWh of gas a year for heating, hot water and any gas cooking, from government meter data. A poorly insulated one uses about 7,770 kWh and a well insulated one about 5,950 kWh.

Why is my bill higher than this?

The usual reasons are a colder than average home, more people in it, being at home in the day, or a tariff above the price cap. Our bill checker compares your own bill with a home like yours.
