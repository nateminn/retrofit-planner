Five questions worth asking every installer

  1. Will you do a room-by-room heat loss survey before quoting, and can I see it?
  2. What design flow temperature is the system sized for, and which radiators change?
  3. Is the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant deducted on the invoice, and who applies for it?
  4. What seasonal efficiency do you expect, and will you show me a running cost estimate?
  5. Who services it, what does that cost, and what does the warranty cover?
