Solar panel payback in Scotland

The short answer

  • 12.3 years to pay back a typical 4 kW south facing system in Scotland, earning about £486 a year
  • About £5,448 left over 25 years after the £6,000 cost, or about £394 with a battery
  • 14.9 years on an east or west roof, and 9.6 years on a 17.5p export rate

Updated 25 September 2026. Every figure on this page comes from our published model; see where our data comes from.

A typical 4 kW solar system in Scotland costs about £6,000, generates about 3,240 kWh a year and brings in about £486 a year in bill savings and export payments, so it pays for itself in about 12.3 years. These are the solar calculator's figures for all of Scotland.

Payback by system size

About 12.3 years for 4 kW. Bigger systems earn more over 25 years but pay back a little more slowly, because more of their output is exported at 12p rather than used at 26.32p.

The data behind this table

South facing roof at a typical pitch, a household using 2,500 kWh a year (a bill of about £860) that is out for about half the day, no battery, 12p export on Outgoing Octopus. Payback uses the middle of each cost range; 0% VAT until 31 March 2027. The 25 year figure allows for the panels losing 0.5% of their output a year.

Roof direction

South is best, but east or west still pays back in about 14.9 years. A north facing roof makes much less and takes about 20.1 years.

With a battery

A battery slows payback to 23 years here. It adds about £176 a year for £4,600, and a battery lasts 10 to 12 years, so it is bought twice over the life of the panels.

Export tariff

The export rate moves payback more than where you live. The same system pays back in 9.6 years at 17.5p and 21.1 years at 4.1p.

Frequently asked questions

How long do solar panels take to pay back in Scotland?

About 12.3 years for a typical 4 kW south facing system costing £6,000, at the October 2026 price cap and a 12p export rate. It earns about £486 a year and leaves about £5,448 over 25 years.

Are solar panels worth it in Scotland?

On a reasonable roof, yes: a 4 kW system pays for itself in about 12.3 years, well inside a 25 year panel life. An east or west roof takes about 14.9 years, and a battery stretches it to 23 years.

How much electricity does a 4 kW solar system make in Scotland?

About 3,240 kWh a year on a south facing roof, using 810 kWh per kW, a cautious regional figure below the PVGIS result for the sunniest spots (Edinburgh 878 kWh per kW). May makes about 5.3 times as much as December.
