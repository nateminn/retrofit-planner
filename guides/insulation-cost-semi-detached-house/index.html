How much does it cost to insulate a semi-detached house?

The short answer

  • £600 to £750 for loft insulation and £2,200 for cavity walls in a semi, at Energy Saving Trust prices
  • About £105 a year saved by cavity wall insulation in a 3 bed semi on gas, a 21 year payback at full price
  • £12,000 to £15,000 for solid walls, which pays best with a grant such as ECO4

Updated 25 September 2026. Every figure on this page comes from our published model; see where our data comes from.

Insulating a semi-detached house costs from about £600 to top up the loft to £12,000 or more for solid walls. Cavity wall insulation costs about £2,200, the Energy Saving Trust's figure for a semi-detached house, and homes that had it fitted used a median 12.0% less gas afterwards.

What each measure costs

Loft first, then the walls. For a 3 bed semi-detached house on gas, cavity wall insulation saves about £105 a year and pays back in 21 years; solid walls save more but cost far more.

The data behind this table

Costs: Energy Saving Trust, typical professional installation before any grant: loft and cavity figures for a semi-detached house (updated 8 May 2026), solid wall figures for a typical home (18 June 2026), suspended floors £1,400 to £2,500 depending on the house (19 May 2026). Savings: the median fall in gas use measured in real homes after each measure (DESNZ NEED Impact of Measures 2026, Table 1: loft 3.2%, cavity wall 12.0%, solid wall 17.3%), applied to a 3 bed semi-detached house with average insulation at the October 2026 cap price of 7.97p per kWh. The loft figure is the median across all loft jobs, most of them top-ups, so insulating a bare loft saves more. Floor savings are the Trust's own estimates. Payback is cost divided by the yearly saving, at full price. Homes heated by oil, LPG or electricity save more in pounds.

Cavity or solid walls?

Check before you price anything. Homes built from the 1930s usually have cavity walls, which are far cheaper to insulate; brickwork showing the short ends of some bricks means solid walls. Your EPC records the wall type.

Solid wall insulation costs about £12,000 fitted inside or £15,000 outside, and saves about £151 a year here, so at full price it only pays with a grant. Read is cavity wall insulation worth it and solid wall insulation cost.

Loft and cavity together

About £2,950 for both. Homes that had loft and cavity wall insulation fitted together used a median 8.4% less gas, about £73 a year here; that is a different group of homes from the single-measure figures, so the two cannot be compared directly.

In our EPC model the loft adds about 7 points and cavity walls about 8, often enough to lift a home a band. Insulating first also shrinks the heat pump a 3 bed semi-detached house needs, from 9 kW at average insulation to 7.5 kW well insulated. Grants that can pay for it

ECO4 until 31 December 2026, then council grants. ECO4 funds insulation for households on qualifying benefits, in homes rated D to G if owned or E to G if privately rented, and the work must be finished by then.

In England a Warm Homes: Local Grant through your council can help households on lower incomes, and insulation carries 0% VAT until 31 March 2027. Landlords can check the 2030 band C rules with the landlord EPC calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to insulate a semi-detached house?

From about £600 to top up the loft to £12,000 for internal solid wall insulation, at Energy Saving Trust prices before any grant. Cavity wall insulation costs about £2,200 for a semi-detached house.

Is cavity wall insulation worth it for a semi-detached house?

Homes that had it fitted used a median 12.0% less gas, about £105 a year for a 3 bed semi-detached house on gas, so at about £2,200 it takes 21 years to pay back at full price. It can be free through ECO4 for households on qualifying benefits, and it adds about 8 EPC points in our model.

Can I get insulation for my semi-detached house free?

Possibly. ECO4 funds insulation for households on qualifying benefits until 31 December 2026, in homes rated D to G if owned or E to G if privately rented, and in England a Warm Homes: Local Grant through the council can help households on lower incomes. Insulation carries 0% VAT until 31 March 2027.
