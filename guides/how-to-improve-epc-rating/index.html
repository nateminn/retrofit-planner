How to Improve Your EPC Rating Cheaply in 2026

The short answer

  • About 7 points from loft insulation at about £750 in a bare loft, one of the best value upgrades
  • About £70 a point for LED lighting and about £90 for smart heating controls, the cheapest per point
  • Free insulation through ECO4 for households on qualifying benefits, until 31 December 2026

One of the best value improvements is loft insulation at about £750 for a bare loft, which adds about 7 EPC points in our points model. LED lighting and heating controls cost even less per point. But the right combination depends on what your home already has. Here is every improvement ranked by cost per EPC point, plus free options for qualifying households.

The pattern is clear: insulation and lighting upgrades give you the most EPC points per pound spent. A new gas boiler, at about £440 a point, costs about the same per point as a heat pump replacing an old gas boiler, about £430 a point once the grant is taken off. Expensive measures like double glazing and solar panels add fewer points relative to their cost, though they offer other benefits like bill savings and comfort.

EPC requirements for landlords in 2026

Band E now, and a band C equivalent standard from 1 October 2030. The maximum fine rises from £5,000 to £30,000 per property per breach, and landlords will have to spend up to £10,000 per property.

Read the detail

Currently, rental properties in England and Wales must have a minimum EPC rating of Band E (39 points). The government confirmed in January 2026 that every privately rented home must meet a band C equivalent standard from 1 October 2030, with no earlier date for new tenancies. It will be measured on the new EPC metrics, fabric performance first.

Today the maximum fine for letting a property below the minimum standard is £5,000 per property. Under the 2030 rules it rises to £30,000 per property per breach.

Landlords will have to spend up to £10,000 per property. If the home still falls short at that cap, they can register a cost cap exemption, which lasts 10 years. See our EPC guide for landlords.

Do not wait for the deadline

Making improvements gradually is cheaper and less disruptive than rushing. Grant funding through ECO4 and council grants is available now, but ECO4 ends on 31 December 2026. A better rating also cuts your tenants' bills.

How to go from Band D to Band C

You need 1 to 14 points, depending on where in Band D you sit. Near the top, LED lighting or smart controls may do it; loft insulation, LEDs and controls add about 12 to 13 points for about £1,200.

Read the detail

Band D covers 55 to 68 points. Band C starts at 69. So you need to gain 1 to 14 points depending on where you are within Band D.

If you are at the top of Band D (65 to 68 points), a single cheap measure might be enough. LED lighting throughout (2 or 3 points for £100 to £300) or smart heating controls (about 3 points for £150 to £400) could push you over the line.

If you are in the middle of Band D (58 to 64 points), you need 5 to 11 points, which usually means two or three measures. Where the loft has under 100mm, loft insulation plus LED lighting and heating controls add about 12 to 13 points for about £1,200.

If you are at the bottom of Band D (55 to 57 points), you need 12 to 14 points. Cavity wall insulation adds about 8 points for about £2,200, so it usually needs pairing with loft insulation or cheaper measures. Or combine loft insulation, LEDs and smart heating controls for about 12 to 13.

How to go from Band E to Band C

You need 15 to 30 points. Loft and cavity wall insulation plus LED lighting and heating controls add about 20 or 21 points for about £3,400, and ECO4 may fund the insulation until 31 December 2026.

Read the detail

Band E covers 39 to 54 points. Reaching Band C (69 points) means gaining 15 to 30 points. This is a bigger jump but still achievable without massive expense.

The most cost-effective route for many Band E homes is: loft insulation (about 7 points), cavity wall insulation (about 8 points), and LED lighting with heating controls (about 5 or 6 together). That is about 20 or 21 points for about £3,400 at full price. Many homes will qualify for free insulation through ECO4 until 31 December 2026, reducing the cost to the lighting and controls.

Homes with solid walls face a harder challenge. Internal solid wall insulation adds about 10 points in our model but costs about £12,000 for a typical 3 bed semi (Energy Saving Trust). It may be available free through ECO4 for qualifying households until 31 December 2026, and councils in England run Warm Homes: Local Grants for lower income households.

Free improvements you might qualify for

ECO4, council Warm Homes: Local Grants, the Boiler Upgrade Scheme and 0% VAT. ECO4 needs a means-tested benefit and a Band D to G home and ends on 31 December 2026; BUS gives £7,500 towards a heat pump.

Read the detail

Several government schemes provide free or heavily subsidised EPC improvements.

ECO4 scheme. If you receive a means-tested benefit such as Universal Credit or Pension Credit and your EPC is Band D, E, F, or G, you may qualify for free loft insulation, cavity wall insulation, solid wall insulation, or a first-time central heating system. Contact your energy supplier to apply, and do it soon: ECO4 ends on 31 December 2026 and the work has to be finished by then. Check your eligibility.

Warm Homes: Local Grant. Run by councils in England for lower income households, it can fund insulation, heat pumps and solar panels. Availability depends on your council.

Boiler Upgrade Scheme. Provides £7,500 towards a heat pump, or £9,000 when it replaces oil or LPG, until March 2027, in England and Wales. In our points model a heat pump adds about 12 points against a gas boiler over 15 years old. Available to homeowners, including landlords, replacing a fossil fuel heating system. Your MCS-certified installer applies on your behalf. Calculate your heat pump costs.

Home Upgrade Grant. This closed on 31 March 2025 and was replaced by the Warm Homes: Local Grant above. See our Home Upgrade Grant guide.

0% VAT. Solar panels, heat pumps, and insulation are zero-rated for VAT until 31 March 2027, after which the 5% reduced rate returns.

Improvements to avoid (poor value for EPC points)

Double glazing or solar panels bought purely for points, and replacing a working boiler. Double glazing costs about £4,800 for about 6 points, so spend the money on insulation instead.

Read the detail

Double glazing purely for EPC. At about £4,800 for a semi-detached house (Energy Saving Trust) and about 6 points in our model, double glazing is one of the worst value improvements for EPC purposes. It improves comfort and reduces noise, but if your only goal is a better EPC rating, spend the money on insulation instead.

Solar panels purely for EPC. Solar adds about 8 points in our model but costs £5,000 to £7,000. The financial return from solar is good, with a 4 kW system paying for itself in about 11 to 12 years on a south facing roof, but as an EPC strategy alone, it is expensive per point. Install solar for the bill savings, not the EPC bump.

Expensive boiler upgrades when insulation is missing. Replacing a working boiler to gain EPC points is rarely cost-effective. Focus on insulation first. If your boiler does need replacing, consider a heat pump instead, which adds about 12 points against an old gas boiler in our model, against about 8 for a new boiler, and qualifies for the £7,500 grant. After the grant a median heat pump for a 3 bed semi costs about £5,200, against about £3,500 for a new gas boiler.

Getting a new EPC assessment

Book a new EPC after the work: it costs £55 to £150 and takes under an hour. A typical two or three bedroom house costs £65 to £95, and the certificate is valid for 10 years.

Read the detail

After making improvements, you need a new EPC assessment to reflect your upgraded rating. A domestic EPC assessment costs £55 to £150, and £65 to £95 for a typical two or three bedroom house, and takes under an hour for most homes. The assessor checks insulation levels, heating system, windows, lighting, and other features.

Find an accredited energy assessor with the government's assessor finder, and check the EPC register for your current certificate first. Make sure the assessor is aware of all improvements you have made, including anything hidden (like cavity wall insulation) that might not be immediately visible.

An EPC is valid for 10 years. You do not need to renew it until you sell or let the property again, but commissioning a new one after improvements can increase property value and demonstrate compliance with minimum standards.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to improve an EPC rating?

The cheapest single job is a hot water cylinder jacket (about £18), which only helps if you have a cylinder. In our points model the best value per point is LED lighting (£100 to £300, 2 or 3 points) and smart heating controls (£150 to £400, about 3 points), then loft insulation (about £750 in a bare loft, about 7 points). Many households qualify for free insulation through ECO4 until 31 December 2026.

What EPC rating do landlords need in 2026?

Currently band E. From 1 October 2030 every privately rented home must meet a band C equivalent standard, and the maximum fine rises from £5,000 to £30,000 per property per breach. Use our EPC calculator to find the cheapest route to compliance.

How much does it cost to go from EPC D to C?

It depends on your starting point within Band D. If you are near the top (65 to 68 points), LED lighting alone (£100 to £300) may be enough. From the bottom of Band D, expect to spend about £1,200 on loft insulation, LED lighting and heating controls, or more if you also need cavity wall insulation (about £2,200). Free options are available through government grants.

Does a new boiler improve EPC rating?

Yes. In our points model a modern condensing gas boiler adds about 8 points when it replaces one over 15 years old. A heat pump adds about 12 against an old gas boiler but only 4 against a newer one, and qualifies for the £7,500 BUS grant. See our boiler vs heat pump comparison for the full cost analysis.

Can I improve my EPC rating without spending money?

Yes, if you qualify for government grants. ECO4 provides free insulation to many households receiving means-tested benefits until 31 December 2026, and councils in England run Warm Homes: Local Grants for lower income households. The Boiler Upgrade Scheme covers £7,500 of a heat pump, or £9,000 when it replaces oil or LPG.

How long does an EPC last?

An EPC is valid for 10 years. After making improvements, commission a new assessment (£55 to £150) to update your rating. You only need a valid EPC when selling or letting the property.
