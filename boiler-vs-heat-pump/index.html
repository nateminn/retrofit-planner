Boiler vs heat pump: the full picture

Replacing a gas boiler is one of the biggest decisions UK homeowners face. A new condensing gas boiler is the familiar option at £1,500 to £3,500. There is no ban on gas boilers in existing homes; from 24 March 2027 new homes in England must have low carbon heating instead. An air source heat pump costs more upfront: most cost £11,100 to £15,600 installed, and the £7,500 grant brings that to about £3,600 to £8,100. Whether it pays back depends mostly on the electricity tariff you run it on.

Running costs compared

Gas costs 7.97p per kWh (Ofgem price cap, October to December 2026) and a condensing boiler averages around 90% efficient once a real heating season is taken into account, below the figure on the badge. Electricity costs 26.32p per kWh, but a heat pump delivers several kWh of heat for each kWh of electricity. Measured over a year in 428 homes by the government's Electrification of Heat trial, the median was 2.78, with the middle half between 2.55 and 3.05. That puts heat at about 8.9p per kWh from gas, and 8.6p to 10.3p from a heat pump on a standard tariff, but only 5.9p to 7.1p on an 18p heat pump tariff. The tariff decides the answer for most gas homes. In a well-insulated home with a properly sized heat pump, running costs are level with or lower than gas.

Why insulation matters so much

Heat pumps operate at lower flow temperatures than gas boilers (35 to 45C vs 60 to 70C). This works well in insulated homes but less efficiently in draughty ones, because the heat pump has to work harder and its COP drops. This is why we always recommend checking your insulation first and improving your EPC rating before installing a heat pump. The order matters: insulate, then install the heat pump.

Frequently asked questions

Is a heat pump cheaper to run than a gas boiler?

On a heat pump tariff, usually yes. On a standard electricity tariff, about the same or a little more: heat costs about 8.9p per kWh from a gas boiler and 8.6p to 10.3p from a heat pump at the efficiencies measured in real homes. On an 18p heat pump tariff that falls to 5.9p to 7.1p.

How much does a heat pump cost compared to a new boiler?

A new gas boiler costs £1,500 to £3,500. Most air source heat pumps cost £11,100 to £15,600 installed, and the £7,500 Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant brings that to about £3,600 to £8,100, so a heat pump usually still costs more upfront.

Will gas boilers be banned?

Not in existing homes. No ban has been set on replacing or repairing a gas boiler in a home that already has one. From 24 March 2027 new homes in England must be built with low carbon heating such as a heat pump, under the Future Homes Standard.

Should I replace my boiler with a heat pump?

Compare the 15 year cost for your home with the calculator above. For many gas homes a heat pump is cheaper over 15 years on a heat pump tariff and dearer on a standard one. Insulate first where you can, because it lowers the running cost of either system.
